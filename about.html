About us

We build calm software for busy founders

Orbitly started in 2021 when three friends got tired of juggling nine tools to run one small company.

OUR STORY · SINCE 2021

We didn't start a company.
We started a better way.

Orbitly began with a simple question: why does running a small business have to feel so complicated? We set out to bring clarity to the everyday chaos of building something meaningful.
