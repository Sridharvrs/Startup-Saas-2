GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things you might be wondering.

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Send us a message and we'll point you in the right direction.

Ask us directly ↗
How quickly will I hear back?

We aim to respond to email enquiries within one working day. Response times can vary depending on the nature of your question.

Can I schedule a product demo?

Absolutely. Choose “Book a product demo” in the contact form and tell us a little about your team. We'll follow up to arrange a suitable time.

Can you help us get started?

Yes. Our Scale plan includes a guided workshop, and you can contact our team to discuss the right onboarding approach for your needs.

Can I ask about pricing before signing up?

Of course. Visit our pricing page or choose “Sales and pricing” in the contact form to discuss your requirements.

Where is Stackly available?

You can use Stackly online from supported locations. Contact us if you have questions about your region or team's requirements.
